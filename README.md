# QuantBox Labs

QuantBox Labs is a quantitative research and proprietary algorithmic trading firm.

The company builds systematic strategies grounded in statistical evidence, rigorous
backtesting, and disciplined risk management. Its first research system, QB-01, focuses
on statistical arbitrage in digital asset markets. Crypto markets were selected as the
initial environment for their data accessibility and low operating cost, not as a
long term positioning. The research infrastructure is designed to extend to other
asset classes over time.


## Principles

QuantBox Labs operates under a small number of non negotiable principles.

Proprietary capital only. The firm does not manage third party funds until an
appropriate legal and regulatory framework has been established.

Research precedes execution. No strategy is deployed with real capital before
demonstrating a statistically credible edge in out of sample backtesting.

Reproducibility is mandatory. Any backtest result that cannot be reproduced from the
same data and the same code is not considered valid.

Risk controls are not optional. No live capital is exposed to the market without
active risk limits and a functioning kill switch.
